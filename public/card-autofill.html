In Chrome, click the card number field and choose a saved payment card. Complete any verification Chrome asks for.

Choose a saved card, then check which fields were filled.
No saved card suggestions?
  1. Open chrome://settings/payments in Chrome and check that saving and filling payment methods is enabled and a card is saved.
  2. Use your regular Chrome profile, then reload this page and click the card number field.
  3. Card autofill needs HTTPS with a certificate trusted by Chrome. Plain HTTP (including localhost) can show the insecure connection warning. For this project's HTTPS test server, use https://localhost:5443/card-autofill.html after its certificate has been trusted.

The browser controls whether it offers autofill. Security code autofill may require verification or manual entry. This tests Chrome card autofill, not a Google Pay checkout.

This page does not send, log, or store your card details, and cannot charge your card. Chrome manages its own saved payment data.
